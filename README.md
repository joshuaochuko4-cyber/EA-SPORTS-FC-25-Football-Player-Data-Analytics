# EA-SPORTS-FC-25-Football-Player-Data-Analytics# 
⚽ EA SPORTS FC 25 – Football Player Data Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores football player data from EA SPORTS FC across FIFA editions 15–24, using SQL/data preparation techniques, Microsoft Excel, and Power BI to transform raw player records into meaningful insights.

The project involved independently exploring the dataset, identifying relevant analytical areas, preparing the data, developing analytical measures, and building a four-page interactive Power BI dashboard.

The objective was to understand player characteristics, ratings, potential, market valuation, wages, and changes across FIFA editions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse player ratings and potential across FIFA editions.
- Examine player age and positional distribution.
- Identify clubs and nationalities with the highest player representation.
- Explore player appearances across multiple FIFA editions.
- Analyse player market values and wages.
- Build an interactive dashboard to communicate findings effectively.

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains:

| Description | Details |
|---|---:|

| Unique player IDs | 53,111 |

**Key dataset fields include:**

- Overall rating
- Potential rating
- Club

- Wage (€)

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.

- **DAX:** Measures and analytical calculations.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data inspection and supporting analysis.

## 🔍 Data Preparation and Methodology

The project followed these stages:

1. **Data Exploration:** Examined the dataset structure, columns, data types, and available FIFA editions.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Reviewed missing values, checked records, and prepared relevant fields for analysis.
3. **Data Transformation:** Selected relevant analytical columns and created a Primary Position field using the first-listed player position.
4. **Player Edition Analysis:** Developed a player-level detail table to examine the number of FIFA editions in which players appeared.
5. **Measure Development:** Created DAX measures for player counts, average ratings, potential, market value, and wages.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Designed a four-page interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 – Player Overview
Provides a high-level overview of the player dataset, including key player statistics and distribution.

### Page 2 – Player and Club Analysis
Explores player positions, club representation, nationalities, and player distribution.

### Page 3 – Player Performance and FIFA Edition Analysis
Examines player appearances across FIFA editions, rating progression, and the relationship between overall ratings and potential.

### Page 4 – Market Value and Wage Analysis
Explores player market values, highest-valued players, wage distribution, and changes in average market value and wages across FIFA editions.

## 📈 Key Analytical Findings

- The dataset contains 180,021 player-edition records representing 53,111 unique player IDs.
- Average player overall rating is 65.71, while average potential rating is 70.78.
- The average player age is approximately 25 years.
- Average market value is approximately €2.38 million.
- The highest recorded market value is €194 million.
- Average recorded player wage is approximately €10,638.
- The highest recorded player wage is €575,000.

*Note: Market values and wages represent the values recorded in the FIFA dataset, not independently verified real-world transfer fees or salaries. A player may appear in multiple FIFA editions.*

## 💡 Business Value and Recommendations

The analysis demonstrates how structured football player data can support:

- Player scouting and comparative performance assessment.
- Identification of players with high potential.
- Exploration of player valuation and wage patterns.
- Club and nationality representation analysis.
- Historical comparison of player attributes across FIFA editions.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Missing values were present in some club, market value, and wage fields.
- Player records represent different FIFA editions and should not automatically be treated as unique individuals.
- Market value and wage figures are dataset-specific.
- The analysis is descriptive and does not establish the causes of changes in player ratings or valuations.
